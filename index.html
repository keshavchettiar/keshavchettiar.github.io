BiteWise AI

Mobile App Development module

Know what's on your plate from a single photo.

Logging meals is tedious, so most people give up on it. With BiteWise, you snap a photo of your food and get an estimate of its calories and macros back, with no ingredients to type in.

Flutter, Firebase, Gemini API, NETS QR, Riverpod

  • AI that reads your meal. Google's Gemini API looks at the photo and works out the macros.
  • Ready to earn. A premium plan paid through NETS QR, Singapore's local payment network. The integration was certified by NETS and Temasek Poly.
  • Progress you can see. A weekly dashboard shows how your eating is trending over time.
  • Easy to start. Sign in with email or Google, and your meal history is saved to the cloud.

Secure Web Portal

Application Security (APSEC) module

Secure access, built in from day one.

A web portal where members, the president and the treasurer each get their own level of access, so people only see what they're supposed to. It's built to hold up against common attacks like password guessing.

Node.js, Express, MongoDB, JWT, bcrypt

  • The right access for the right people. Three roles with their own permissions, designed around OWASP security guidelines.
  • Passwords done properly. Hashed and salted with bcrypt, with strength rules and secure JWT sessions.
  • Stops brute-force attacks. Accounts lock after 5 failed logins, and login attempts are rate limited.
  • Self-service recovery. Forgot your password? A reset link is emailed straight to you.
