Things I've built

Two projects I'm proud of. Happy to walk through the code for either one.

BiteWise AI

A nutrition tracker for your phone

Certified by NETS

Flutter, Dart, Firebase, Gemini API, NETS QR, Riverpod

  • Snap a photo of your meal and the app estimates the macros using Google's Gemini API, so you don't have to type in every ingredient.
  • I integrated NETS QR payments for a premium subscription, including sandbox transactions and tracking payment status. This earned a certificate from NETS and Temasek Polytechnic, co-signed by NETS' Managing Director.
  • Login works with email or Google Sign-In through Firebase, and meal history is stored in Firestore. Riverpod handles app state.
  • A weekly dashboard shows your nutrition trends so you can see how you're doing over time.

APSEC

A secure web app for a club

Graded A

Node.js, Express, MongoDB, JWT, bcrypt

  • Three roles (Member, President and Treasurer), each seeing only what they're allowed to, built following OWASP guidelines.
  • Passwords are hashed and salted with bcrypt, must meet strength rules, and logins use JWT sessions.
  • Accounts lock after 5 failed attempts and login requests are rate limited, which stops brute-force attacks.
  • Forgot your password? A reset link gets emailed to you through Nodemailer.
