BiteWise AI

Mobile App Development module, certified by NETS

A nutrition tracking app. You take a photo of your meal and it uses Google's Gemini API to estimate the calories and macros, so you don't have to type in every ingredient.

It's by far my proudest project. I added a premium subscription paid through NETS QR, and getting the sandbox payments and payment status working took a fair bit of effort. That part ended up getting a certificate from NETS and TP, signed by NETS' Managing Director.

Flutter, Dart, Firebase, Gemini API, NETS QR API, Riverpod
  • Login with email or Google, using Firebase Authentication
  • Meal history saved in Firestore
  • A weekly dashboard that shows how your nutrition is trending
  • Riverpod for managing app state

Secure Web Portal

Application Security (APSEC) module, Grade A

A web app with three kinds of users: members, the president and the treasurer. Each one can only see and do what their role allows. The module was all about making apps hard to break into, so most of my time went into the login and account side.

Node.js, Express, MongoDB, JWT, bcrypt
  • Passwords are hashed and salted with bcrypt, and have to meet strength rules
  • JWT for login sessions
  • An account locks after 5 failed login attempts, and login requests are rate limited, so brute-forcing a password doesn't work
  • Forgot your password? It emails you a reset link (Nodemailer)
  • I followed the OWASP guidelines throughout
